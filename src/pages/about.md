Stephen is a Black engineer and community leader in open source, based in New
York City.

He is the Lead Architect for Open Source Infrastructure & Security at
[Bloomberg][bloomberg].

He currently participates in project-level steering committees
([darnit][darnit], [OpenSSF Scorecard][scorecard], [TODO Group][todo]) and
helps maintain a few codebases around open source projects you may have heard
of.

Additionally, he serves as an advisor and investor for startups in the open
source ecosystem.

Within the Open Source Security Foundation ([OpenSSF][ossf]), he serves on the
Governing Board as a General Member Representative and on the Technical
Advisory Council. He chairs the [Supply Chain Integrity Working Group][sci],
OpenSSF's front door for helping maintainers adopt supply chain security.

He is a core maintainer for [OpenSSF Scorecard][scorecard] and its
[Allstar][allstar] subproject. Scorecard scans more than 1.3 million
repositories every week to help assess the security posture of open source
projects.

For [Kubernetes][k8s], he serves as a chair of [SIG Release][sig-release] and
is an emeritus member of the [Steering Committee][k8s-steering].
He co-created foundational parts of the project, including the
[KEP][enhancements] (Kubernetes Enhancements Proposal) process, the
[Release Engineering][releng] subproject, and Working Group Naming. He has
previously served as a chair of both SIG PM and SIG Azure.

Across the wider Cloud Native Computing Foundation ([CNCF][cncf]) ecosystem, he
is a former [TAG Contributor Strategy][contrib-strat] chair, [Dex][dex]
maintainer, and program chair for [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon][kubecon], the
cloud native community's flagship conference.

He is a [prolific contributor][devstats] to CNCF projects and among the top 100
pull request authors of the past decade.

In 2020, he co-founded the [Inclusive Naming Initiative][ini], a
cross-industry group dedicated to helping projects and companies make
consistent, responsible choices to remove harmful language across codebases,
standards, and documentation.

Previously, he was Head of Open Source at Cisco, where he founded the Open
Source Program Office (OSPO). He has also worked at VMware (via Heptio), Red
Hat, and CoreOS, and has served on the governing boards of the [CNCF][cncf]
and the [OpenAPI Initiative][oapi].
